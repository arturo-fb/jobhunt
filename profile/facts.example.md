# Facts the cover letters may use

Copy this file to `profile/facts.md` and replace every line with your own facts.
Your AI assistant uses ONLY what is written here (plus your resume in `master/`),
so a letter can never claim something you didn't put down. Keep it factual and
specific: numbers, dates, names of programs and awards.

## Education
- Degree, major(s), university, GPA, graduation month/year
- Honors, scholarships, relevant coursework

## Experience
- Role, organization, dates — one or two accomplishments with numbers
- Role, organization, dates — ...

## Projects, competitions, leadership
- e.g. stock pitch competition result, investment club, research project

## Certifications and skills
- e.g. licenses or exams passed, software, programming languages, languages spoken

## Work authorization (only mentioned if a posting asks)
- e.g. US citizen / permanent resident / F-1 with STEM OPT
